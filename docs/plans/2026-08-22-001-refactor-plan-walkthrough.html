User flows after landing

Every number in these flows is exact at origination, and the CS4 facts label will render them verbatim.

Deposit PT vault · one approval
  1. You approve PT once, and the fee comes out of the mint so there is nothing else to approve.
  2. The vault pulls PT, reads the TWAP rate, and splits immediate versus streamed portions.
  3. It mints toUser minus fee to you, sends the fee to treasury, and streams the discount.
  4. You receive ovrfloTokens plus an OVRFLO Stream NFT, and the deposit assert runs first.
Approval count falls from two to one because fee payment now travels inside the mint itself.
Wrap underlying OVRFLOReserve · permissionless · no fee
  1. You send underlying to OVRFLOReserve, which mints 1:1 forever with no tin fee, no tout fee, and no ceiling.
  2. wrappedUnderlying accounting moves over from the audited code unchanged, byte for byte.
  3. An end-of-call assert pins wrappedUnderlying at or under balance, so the peg gets checked rather than assumed.
  4. Three labeled exits exist: unwrap is a window, DEX works anytime, claim is guaranteed.
Because OVRFLOReserve asserts the peg on every call, other contracts stop needing to assume it.
Lend lending · ovrfloToken
  1. You get ovrfloTokens by deposit, wrap, or DEX, then sign a permit and skip the approve transaction.
  2. You supply at an APR tick; resting liquidity withdraws anytime while filled liquidity locks to maturity.
  3. Fills pay you in ovrfloToken, claims floor pro rata, and rounding dust strands in proceeds by design.
  4. Collect-and-relend compounds your position without ever leaving the asset.
Lenders post ovrfloToken and borrowers draw ovrfloToken, which makes this a single-asset book.
Borrow lending · nonrecourse
  1. You pledge a stream, and the fill crosses cheapest ticks blind with gas flat as positions accumulate.
  2. Proceeds arrive in ovrfloToken net of fee, and the obligation ceils toward the lender.
  3. Through the router path (CS3), the book escrows your stream and calls borrow with onBehalfOf set to you.
  4. Loan, proceeds, and stream return are all yours because the router holds nothing after execute.
Once CS3 ships, unmet demand rests visibly on chain, and attribution keeps streams off middlemen entirely.

Decisions that shape it

Each decision below carries the alternative it rejected along with the reason that alternative lost.

KD5 deploy chain
Vault constructs OVRFLOReserve; OVRFLOReserve constructs the token. One transaction, zero prediction.
CREATE2 offers no computable fixed point here, nonce-precomputed CREATE stays silent until registration reverts, and both lose to nested construction.
KD4 minters
Two immutable authorities named vault() and reserve(), both mint and burn, plus ERC20Permit.
Numbered slots such as minter0/minter1 buy nothing because the handoff forbids any third minter outright.
KD10 borrower identity
Factory-set router slot. borrow attributes onBehalfOf. Events emit the human, never the router.
Per-user operator maps guard a harm that cannot occur, settle tables strand NFTs on the router, and both cost more bytes than the slot.
KD7 repairability
replaceLending repoints a column. The old market stays known while its loans wind down.
Unregister and pause switches stay out of scope, and vault-level replace remains excluded because pattern #9 protects token fungibility.
KD2 fees
Fee deducted from the mint. Event, slippage guard, and preview all report net.
A gross-bound slippage check lost because users must bound the amount they actually receive.
KD8 frei-pi
Peg asserts on wrap and unwrap. Deposit assert on the vault. Borrow skips; structure already enforces it.
The roughly 2,600-gas-per-call cost is accepted in exchange for turning the peg into an on-chain fact.
KD11 generation
Fresh generation including a new lockup from the sibling repo. Devnets and testnets reseed.
Constructor-level wiring rules out in-place migration entirely, so mainnet launch stays blocked until CS1 completes.
KD6 registry abi
OvrfloInfo tuple frozen. OVRFLOReserve lives beside it in ovrfloToReserve. Registration keeps one argument.
The off-repo stream mint gate reads that tuple positionally, which is why it must not grow.
